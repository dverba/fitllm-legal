FitLLM — Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 10, 2026

FitLLM (“the App”, “we”, “us”) is developed by Verba Labs. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your choices.

In plain language: FitLLM is an AI fitness coach. To coach you, it reads health and workout data from Apple Health and (optionally) Hevy, and sends relevant derived information to an AI service to generate personalized guidance. Your data stays on your device except as described below. We never use your health data for advertising and never sell it.

1. Information we collect

  • Apple Health (HealthKit), with your permission: workouts, heart rate, active energy, steps, body mass (weight), height, VO₂ Max, date of birth, and biological sex.
  • Hevy data, only if you connect Hevy: routines, exercises, sets, reps, weights, and completed-workout history. Your Hevy API key is stored securely in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • Information you provide: age, weight, height, sex, fitness goals, available equipment, injuries or constraints, preferences, and the messages you send to the AI coach.
  • Account information: when you sign in with Apple, Google, or email, we receive an account identifier and, depending on the method, your name and email address.
  • Diagnostic & usage data: during the beta, we log the requests sent to and responses received from the AI service (including the health-derived content of those requests), along with technical metadata such as timestamps, latency, and which AI model was used. This helps us debug and improve coaching quality.

2. How we use your information

  • To generate personalized coaching, plans, weekly reviews, and chat responses.
  • To track your progress and tailor recommendations over time.
  • To diagnose problems and improve the App during the beta.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. How your information is shared

We share data only with service providers that help us operate the App:

  • AI provider (OpenRouter and the AI model providers it routes to): to generate coaching, we send prompts containing health-derived and workout information; these providers process it to return a response and may temporarily retain it under their own privacy policies.
  • Supabase: our backend provider, which handles sign-in and stores the diagnostic/usage data above on our behalf, protected by access controls and encrypted in transit.
  • Hevy: if you connect Hevy, the App communicates directly with Hevy’s API using your key, subject to Hevy’s privacy policy.
  • Apple: Apple Health data comes from your device; Sign in with Apple is handled by Apple.

We may also disclose information where required by law. We never sell your data, and we never use Apple Health (HealthKit) data — or share it — for advertising, marketing, or data-mining purposes.

4. Where your data is stored

  • Most of your data (profile, plans, history, chat) is stored locally on your device. We do not store your Apple Health data in iCloud.
  • Diagnostic/usage data is stored on Supabase servers.
  • Data in transit is protected with TLS/HTTPS.

5. Data retention

We retain diagnostic/usage data for the duration of the beta program, and no longer than 12 months, after which it is deleted or anonymized. Data stored locally on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the App. When you delete your account, we delete the associated server-side data as described below.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Permissions: You control Apple Health access in the iOS Health app and can revoke it at any time. Hevy is optional and can be disconnected.
  • Access & correction: You can view and edit your profile data in the App.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated server-side data at any time from Profile → Delete Account in the App. This is immediate and permanent. If you're unable to access the App, you can also request deletion by emailing privacy@verba.nyc.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA; contact us to exercise them.

7. Children

FitLLM is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above and, where appropriate, notifying you in the App.
